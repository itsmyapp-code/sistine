## LOG A: Statutory Data Protection Officer (DPO) Need Assessment

### Assessment Overview
Under UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) Article 37(1) and Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) regulatory guidelines, an organization must determine whether the mandatory appointment of a statutory Data Protection Officer (DPO) is legally required.

### Legal Criteria Review:
1. **Public Authority or Body (Article 37(1)(a)):**
   - *Finding:* Apex Decorating & Restoration is a private sole proprietorship / limited trade company providing painting, decorating, and property restoration services. It is **not** a public authority or body.
2. **Core Activities Involving Regular and Systematic Monitoring of Data Subjects on a Large Scale (Article 37(1)(b)):**
   - *Finding:* The business processes data solely for the purpose of communicating with prospective clients, issuing estimates and invoices, arranging site visits, and maintaining customer service records. It does **not** conduct large-scale tracking, behavioral profiling, or systematic monitoring of data subjects.
3. **Core Activities Consisting of Large Scale Processing of Special Categories of Data or Criminal Convictions (Article 37(1)(c)):**
   - *Finding:* The business does not collect or process special category data (e.g. biometric, health, philosophical, religious data) or criminal records.

### Conclusion & Statutory Determination:
> **Determination:** Apex Decorating & Restoration is **not legally mandated** under UK GDPR Article 37(1) to appoint a formal statutory Data Protection Officer (DPO).
